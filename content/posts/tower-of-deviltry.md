[Tower of Deviltry](https://ill-gotten-games.itch.io/tower-of-deviltry) is a one-page RPG by Ill Gotten Games. It is a game I have played with a friend, and while reading the rules took a while to fully grasp it, playing it in the end was kind of fun. For such a simple game, it does offer a lot of depth and replayability. To make things easier, I created these random generators to help with character and map generation.

These are meant to be printed and played with pen and paper. Or download as an image and use it in a VTT, like Owlbear Rodeo.
